Could Scott Bain Become Scotland's First-Choice Goalkeeper Under Sebastien Pocognoli?

9/6/2026 · By Koora Live

Scott Bain’s name has surfaced prominently in discussions about Scotland’s goalkeeping future. With new national team manager Sebastien Pocognoli aiming to redefine Scotland's playing identity, questions are being raised: is Bain the ideal choice to protect the Scottish goalpost?

Sebastien Pocognoli's Vision for Scotland

Sebastien Pocognoli, widely respected for his time as a player and coach, is now tasked with reshaping Scotland’s national team. Known for his preference for modern, proactive football, he has often championed a playing style that is possession-oriented and relies on technical precision. This represents a shift from Scotland's traditionally robust and defensive approach. In such a system, the role of a goalkeeper becomes much more significant, not just in shot-stopping but also in starting attacks and building from the back. This transformation begs the question of whether the current options available in Scotland’s goalkeeping roster align with Pocognoli’s ideals.

Why Scott Bain Could Be Pocognoli's Solution

Scott Bain has steadily established himself as a reliable presence in goal for club and country. Currently playing for Celtic, Bain has demonstrated his composure under pressure, excellent distribution, and ability to launch counter-attacks with pinpoint passing—abilities that are increasingly sought after in goalkeepers under modern football systems. Bain has also shown tactical awareness, often positioning himself as a ‘sweeper-keeper,’ ready to step off his line to clear danger when necessary. If Pocognoli is keen on implementing a style that prioritizes retention and a structured build-up from the back, Bain's skill set may make him the natural choice to fill that role for Scotland.

Analysis

The focus on Scott Bain possibly becoming Scotland’s number one highlights a broader strategic shift under Sebastien Pocognoli. Analysts may interpret Bain’s inclusion in discussions as a signal of Pocognoli’s commitment to a progressive football philosophy. To succeed, Bain will not only need to excel in performances for his club but also convince Pocognoli that he can serve as the cornerstone of Scotland’s revamped style. While Bain has the technical abilities, challenging competition from Scotland's other goalkeeping options cannot be overlooked. Player form, fitness, and tactical understanding will likely influence Pocognoli’s ultimate decision.

As Sebastien Pocognoli looks to overhaul the Scotland national team’s style of play, Scott Bain's abilities could provide a compelling case for his inclusion as the first-choice goalkeeper. However, the final decision will depend on how Bain rises to the challenge in the coming months.
