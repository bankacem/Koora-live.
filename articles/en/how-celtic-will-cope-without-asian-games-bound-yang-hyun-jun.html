The Asian Games and its Importance

The Asian Games is a major sporting event held every four years, featuring athletes from across Asia competing in a variety of disciplines. While often overlooked in Europe, the football competition is highly prestigious in Asia, offering an opportunity for younger talents to shine on the international stage. Yang Hyun-jun’s participation is a significant moment in his career, as it could serve to further establish him as a key figure for South Korea. For Celtic, however, his temporary absence poses a test of squad depth and resilience as the fixtures pile up in what is always a demanding season for the Glasgow giants.
