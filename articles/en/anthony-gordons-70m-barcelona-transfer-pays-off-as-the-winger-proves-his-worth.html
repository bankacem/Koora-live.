Anthony Gordon’s £70m Barcelona Transfer Pays Off as The Winger Proves His Worth

9/12/2026 · By Koora Live

When Anthony Gordon signed for FC Barcelona in the summer for a reported £70 million, a flicker of doubt lingered in the press and amongst some fans. Some commentators questioned whether a player who had yet to fully break into the England senior setup could fill the void left by Marcus Rashford, especially after the English striker’s recent injury struggles. Yet, according to BBC Sport, the young winger is already making an impact for the free‑scoring Spanish giants.

Barcelona, a club steeped in history and known for its attacking flair, has for years been a magnet for talented wide men. Gordon’s arrival follows a path that many top clubs believe suits his blend of pace, dribbling and off‑the‑ball movement. While the full extent of his contribution will unfold over the coming seasons, early signs point to a player who has begun to weave himself into the tactical tapestry of Pep Guardiola’s side.

While not yet part of the England senior squad, Gordon’s performances for the U‑21 teams, as well as his heroics on the sidelines in 2024, earned him a place on the radar of Barcelona’s scouting network. Experts at the club considered his track record a perfect fit for a side that relies heavily on quick transitions and aerial threats on the break. After a gapped period ahead of the Carrascalan, Gordon has found his footing in league training, his energy and style echoing the team's traditional “tiki‑taka” system, as well as the modern “gegenpress” approach under Guardiola.

Analysis

From a managerial standpoint, Gordon’s early integration signals alignment with Barcelona’s long‑term player philosophy – youth, attract tradition, and adaptability. While it is still early to judge the full return on the £70m investment, the winger’s ability to orchestrate play from wide positions and produce opportunities off the ball resonates with Guardiola’s proactive tactical demands.

The transfer’s success will ultimately revolve around consistency and endurance, qualities Gordon has displayed in training and increasingly in competitive fixtures. Analysts might view this positive trajectory as a hint that Barcelona’s decision to invest in England’s rising star was a strategic bet that will likely pay dividends in the coming seasons, especially as the club seeks to balance seasoned veterans with fresh talent.

With a hefty price tag and initial skepticism, Anthony Gordon’s present contributions affirm the wisdom behind Barcelona’s decision. As the season progresses, the winger’s continued performance will decide whether the £70 million investment grows beyond a promising gambit into a lasting asset for the Catalan giants.
