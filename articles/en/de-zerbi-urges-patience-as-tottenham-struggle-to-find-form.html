De Zerbi Urges Patience as Tottenham Struggle to Find Form

9/5/2026 · By Koora Live

Roberto de Zerbi has called for patience as Tottenham endure a frustrating start to the Premier League season. With three consecutive games without scoring, the Spurs manager believes it will take time to mold his costly squad into a cohesive team.

Tottenham's Early Season Struggles

Tottenham Hotspur have made a stuttering start to their 2023/24 Premier League campaign, with a failure to find the back of the net in their opening three fixtures. This goal drought has raised questions among fans and analysts, especially given the expectations that surrounded the squad following significant financial investment in recent transfer windows. Traditionally known for their attacking talent and creativity, the current lack of goals has highlighted the challenges faced during this transitional period under Roberto de Zerbi's management.

The Challenge of Building a Team

Following the departure of key players in recent seasons and the influx of new signings, De Zerbi has cautioned against expecting immediate results. Drawing attention to the complexities of integrating an expensive squad, he emphasized that building a team capable of competing at the highest level requires time and patience. Tottenham’s situation is not unfamiliar in modern football, where rebuilding cycles often necessitate perseverance from both the management and supporters. De Zerbi’s message seems to reflect a long-term vision for the club, signaling his commitment to creating not just a competitive team, but a cohesive unit.

Analysis

The current state of Tottenham Hotspur is emblematic of the challenges that come with transitioning under a new managerial philosophy. Roberto de Zerbi, renowned for his success at Brighton, faces the complicated task of aligning the club's high-spending strategy with his tactical ideals. While frustration over the scoreless start is understandable among fans, the manager's plea for time resonates with the broader reality of football – that instant success is rarely sustainable. Whether Spurs can turn things around in the short term or not, the focus may need to shift to building a strong foundation for future campaigns. Analysts might note that even some of the most successful Premier League managers have required seasons to establish their methods and achieve tangible results.

Tottenham's scoreless start to the season has sparked concern, but Roberto de Zerbi’s emphasis on patience underscores the importance of allowing time for his vision to take shape. Whether this struggle represents growing pains or a deeper issue will become clearer as the campaign progresses.
