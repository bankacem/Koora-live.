Hull City’s Promising Premier League Start: Can They Avoid Relegation?

9/6/2026 · By Koora Live

Hull City, newly promoted to the Premier League, have surprised many by earning seven points from their opening three matches. While it is early to draw firm conclusions about their survival chances, the Tigers seem to have built a strong foundation for their campaign, as highlighted by BBC Sport.

A Solid Start Against the Odds

Returning to the top flight after promotion is never an easy transition, yet Hull City have hit the ground running. By securing seven points from a possible nine in their first three games, they have not only defied early-season expectations but also given themselves a buffer in their quest to avoid a swift return to the Championship. Early momentum in the Premier League is often seen as essential for newly promoted sides, who tend to face a grueling fight for survival throughout the long campaign.

The Significance of a Strong Foundation

A promising start to a Premier League season can set the tone for the rest of the year, particularly for newly promoted teams. Last season, clubs like Brentford exemplified how a fast start helps establish a much-needed points cushion against potential mid-season struggles. Furthermore, Hull City’s current form will build confidence in their squad, which may not boast the depth or experience of more established Premier League teams.

Analysis

While it may be premature to predict Hull City’s survival outright, their solid start is a positive indicator. History tells us that teams who manage to reach or surpass the 40-point mark usually retain their Premier League status. Having already collected seven points, Hull have made a significant step in that direction. However, tougher tests lie ahead, and it will be crucial for them to maintain consistency in performance and results. Rotations and injuries could test the depth of their squad, which is often a challenge for newly promoted sides.

Hull City’s start to the season has undoubtedly given fans hope that they can secure Premier League survival. However, the campaign is a marathon, not a sprint, and the true test lies in their ability to sustain this momentum over the coming months. For now, their performance has laid a positive foundation upon which they can look to build.
