Rangers Face Goal Drought After 'Significant' Shankland Injury

9/6/2026 · By Koora Live

Rangers' attacking options have taken a major hit, with news emerging that captain Lawrence Shankland is set for a spell on the sidelines. The striker suffered what is described as a 'significant' hamstring injury during the Scottish Premiership victory over Motherwell on Saturday, raising questions about who will fill the void upfront.

Lawrence Shankland Sidelined with Hamstring Injury

Lawrence Shankland, a key figure in Rangers' attacking setup, has been dealt a significant setback. BBC Sport reports that the 28-year-old captain sustained a serious hamstring injury during Rangers' recent clash with Motherwell, which saw the Glasgow giants secure a victory. While further details about the severity and timeline of his recovery remain under wraps, hamstring injuries often require weeks, if not months, of rehabilitation depending on their severity. This development leaves Rangers with a pressing issue in the goalscoring department, as Shankland's contributions have been vital thus far.

Rangers' Goal-Scoring Options Without Shankland

With Shankland ruled out, Rangers manager Philippe Clement must now explore alternative attacking strategies. As the team's talisman and captain, Shankland's impact is more than just about goals; his leadership on the pitch and ability to link up play leave a void that is not easy to fill. The remaining options in the squad, including young prospects or seasoned substitutes, will likely need to step up in the upcoming fixtures. Additionally, set-piece specialists and midfield contributors could find themselves tasked with increasing their attacking output to compensate for Shankland's absence.

Analysis

Shankland’s injury presents a key challenge for Rangers as they look to maintain momentum in a highly competitive Scottish Premiership season.
